ARI CIRCLE

Community guidelines

Be real, be respectful, and help keep ARI Circle safe.

AGE & TEEN CIRCLE

ARI Circle is 13+.

Children under 13 cannot use ARI Circle. Accounts ages 13–17 use Teen Circle protections and are separated from adult discovery and matching. Do not misrepresent your age or try to bypass an age-based safety control.

TEEN PRIVACY

Keep teen connections inside ARI Circle.

Teen accounts may not share or request phone numbers, email addresses, external links, off-app social handles, exact addresses or locations, or private in-person meetup plans. Do not pressure another teen to move a conversation off ARI Circle or reveal where they live.

TEEN BUDDIES

Interest-based, not location-based.

Teen Buddies is limited to teen-only group or accountability discovery. Adult matches, adult activities, precise-area matching, and 1-on-1 Buddy discovery are not available to teen accounts.

RESPECT PEOPLE

No harassment or hate.

Do not bully, threaten, shame, stalk, or target anyone based on identity or protected characteristics.

PROTECT PRIVACY

Share only what is yours.

Do not publish private information, intimate images, access credentials, or another person's personal data without clear permission.

KEEP IT AUTHENTIC

No scams, spam, or impersonation.

Do not deceive people for money, imitate another person, manipulate engagement, or send repetitive unwanted messages.

SAFETY FIRST

No illegal or dangerous exploitation.

Content that exploits minors, promotes credible violence, facilitates serious wrongdoing, or encourages self-harm is not allowed.

BEFORE YOU POST

ARI Circle screens submissions.

ARI Circle may screen text, photos, and sampled frames from short videos before publication to help filter harmful or objectionable material. Teen-safety rules can also block contact information, exact-location sharing, and meetup coordination. Screening can make mistakes, so reporting and owner review remain important.

ENFORCEMENT

Blocked attempts can be reviewed.

Safety violations may be blocked before publication. Relevant teen-safety events can be retained in a limited, redacted owner review queue so repeated or serious behavior can be investigated without exposing complete private conversations.

USE THE TOOLS

Block and report.

Block a profile to stop interaction. Report a person, post, or technical problem so the ARI XP owner can review it privately. Deliberately false or abusive reports may also violate these guidelines.
