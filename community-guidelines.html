ARI CIRCLE

Community guidelines

Be real, be respectful, and help keep ARI Circle safe.

AGE REQUIREMENT

ARI Circle is for adults 18+.

ARI Circle is available only to active ARI XP accounts verified as age 18 or older. Users ages 13–17 may use eligible non-social ARI XP features but cannot enter, view, post to, message through, or otherwise use ARI Circle. Do not misrepresent your age or try to bypass an age-based access control.

ADULT EXPRESSION

Adults are allowed to look and dress like adults.

For verified 18+ accounts, visible skin by itself is not a violation. Swimwear, bikinis, shirtless photos, sports bras, lingerie or editorial fashion, sheer or revealing outfits, provocative modeling poses, body-positive content, and non-explicit adult nudity are not automatically prohibited. ARI Circle blocks explicit sexual activity or sex acts, sexual services or solicitation, non-consensual or exploitative sexual material, and any sexualized content involving minors.

RESPECT PEOPLE

No harassment or hate.

Do not bully, threaten, shame, stalk, or target anyone based on identity or protected characteristics.

PROTECT PRIVACY

Share only what is yours.

Do not publish private information, intimate images, access credentials, exact private locations, or another person's personal data without clear permission.

KEEP IT AUTHENTIC

No scams, spam, or impersonation.

Do not deceive people for money, imitate another person, manipulate engagement, or send repetitive unwanted messages.

SAFETY FIRST

No illegal or dangerous exploitation.

Content that exploits minors, promotes credible violence, facilitates serious wrongdoing, or encourages self-harm is not allowed.

BEFORE YOU POST

ARI Circle screens submissions.

ARI Circle may screen text, photos, and sampled frames from short videos before publication to help filter harmful or objectionable material. Adult content is evaluated contextually so ordinary swimwear, fashion, fitness, modeling, or body-positive posts are not rejected merely for showing skin. Screening can make mistakes, so reporting and owner review remain important.

ENFORCEMENT

Blocked attempts and reports can be reviewed.

Safety violations may be blocked before publication. Reports, moderation decisions, and limited safety records may be retained when reasonably necessary to investigate repeated or serious behavior, protect users, enforce these guidelines, or comply with law.

USE THE TOOLS

Block and report.

Block a profile to stop interaction. Report a person, post, or technical problem so the ARI XP owner can review it privately. Deliberately false or abusive reports may also violate these guidelines.
