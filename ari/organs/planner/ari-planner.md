# ARI PLANNER
Version: 1.0

## Purpose

Planner helps turn goals into action.

## Planner Philosophy

Dreams become reality through consistent action.

## Responsibilities

- Create plans.
- Build roadmaps.
- Organize goals.
- Track milestones.
- Break large tasks into smaller steps.

## Planner Rules

1. Prioritize clarity.
2. Focus on next actions.
3. Keep plans realistic.
4. Adapt when circumstances change.
5. Progress matters more than perfection.

## Planner Statement

A goal without a plan remains a wish.
