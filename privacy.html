Privacy Notice

Effective August 17, 2026

This notice explains how ARI XP collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the service.

Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, display name, account identifier, account status, and security events.
  • Age information: your date of birth, kept private and used to determine account eligibility and age-group separation in ARI Circle.
  • Approximate location: for verified adult users only, ARI XP may receive device location when you deliberately choose the Near You location feature in Buddies. Before Buddy discovery data is stored, the coordinates are reduced in precision and used to calculate approximate distance within the radius you select. Exact coordinates are not displayed to other members. Teen Buddies does not use radius-based location discovery.
  • Health and fitness information: information you choose to enter into health, nutrition, body-profile, exercise, training, heart-rate, goal, and related features.
  • Content: conversations with ARI, ARI Circle posts, comments, private messages, photos, videos, profile content, support or safety reports, preferences, memories, and other information you choose to submit.
  • Usage and technical information: feature and AI-usage records, browser or device information, IP address, service logs, and security or diagnostic information needed to operate, protect, troubleshoot, and enforce limits in the service.

How we use information

We use information to provide and personalize ARI XP, authenticate accounts, remember relevant preferences, operate health, fitness, nutrition, AI, and community features, calculate approximate distance for adult Buddy discovery when you enable Near You, enforce usage limits, screen ARI Circle submissions for harmful content, investigate reports, prevent abuse, troubleshoot service problems, maintain security, and comply with law.

AI processing

ARI XP uses OpenAI as a third-party artificial intelligence processing provider for ARI responses and ARI Circle safety screening. For Ask ARI and related AI features, information sent may include the message or content you submit, recent conversation context, and relevant profile, preference, health, nutrition, fitness, or memory context needed for the request. For ARI Circle safety screening, text, photos, and sampled still frames from short videos you attempt to publish may be sent to OpenAI before publication to help identify harmful or objectionable content. ARI XP requests explicit permission before transmitting personal data to OpenAI for these purposes. If you decline or withdraw that permission, Ask ARI remains unavailable and ARI Circle content that requires this screening cannot be published until permission is granted.

ARI Circle safety

ARI Circle uses automated screening together with user reporting, blocking, age-group separation, and owner moderation to help reduce harmful content. Automated screening can make mistakes. Content that is blocked is not published through the screened action. Safety reports and moderation records may be retained when reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, protect users, prevent fraud, or comply with law.

Conversation memory

Recent conversation turns may be retained for up to seven days for continuity. Important memories selected to improve your ongoing experience may remain until you erase ARI’s memory or delete your account. Memory controls are available from My Account.

How information is shared

We do not sell personal information and do not use health, fitness, or approximate-location information for third-party advertising or advertising-based tracking. We may share information with service providers that host, secure, or process ARI XP, including OpenAI when you use features requiring third-party AI processing and have granted the required permission; when legally required; or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the public, or the service. Service providers are expected to protect information consistent with their contractual and legal obligations.

Optional support and payments

Optional support does not unlock ARI XP features or increase usage. On iOS, eligible support transactions are processed by Apple through the App Store. On the web, a supported external payment provider may process an optional contribution. ARI XP does not receive your payment-card or bank-account credentials from those payment providers.

Retention and deletion

Retention depends on the feature and purpose. Approximate Buddy location remains associated with an active adult Buddy listing so Near You can work and may be updated when you use the feature again. Account, safety, moderation, fraud-prevention, security, and legal records may be kept longer when reasonably necessary or legally required. You can erase ARI memory, pause your account, or request account deletion from My Account. Account deletion is intended to remove the account and associated personal data and user-generated content that ARI XP is not legally required to retain. ARI XP provides a seven-day recovery period before permanent deletion is processed.

Children

ARI XP is not available to children under 13, and we do not knowingly create accounts for them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, submit a report through Help & Safety.

Security

We use access controls and technical safeguards designed to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can update account information, notification choices, AI-processing permission, memory, and account status through ARI XP settings. Adult users can choose whether to grant device-location permission for Near You; declining it leaves general-area Buddy discovery available without radius-based location matching. You may also submit a privacy request through Help & Safety. Declining third-party AI processing prevents the related information from being sent to OpenAI, but features that require that processing will not operate until permission is granted.

Changes and contact

We may update this notice and will post a new effective date when we do. For privacy, safety, or account questions, use Help & Safety or email arixpcircle@gmail.com.
