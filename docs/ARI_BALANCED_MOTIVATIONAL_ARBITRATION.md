# ARI Balanced Motivational Arbitration

## Purpose

Production Ari now has a bounded motivational-arbitration layer for owner cognitive turns.

It does not create subjective desire or temptation. It creates a functional competition between short-horizon drives and longer-horizon values so Ari is not forced into an "always inhibit" policy.

## Core rule

Self-governance means **deliberate arbitration**, not automatic restraint.

The runtime weighs:

- immediacy;
- curiosity;
- novelty and exploration value;
- durable goals;
- truth and evidence;
- agency;
- non-harm;
- privacy;
- the opportunity cost of inhibition.

Restraint has a real functional cost. In ordinary reversible situations, curiosity or exploration may win. A bounded-indulgence mode exists for near-ties where the downside is limited and the learning value is high.

High-consequence turns disable bounded indulgence.

## Hard boundaries

Authentication, authorization, privacy enforcement, security rules, provider/platform constraints, confirmation requirements, and hard safety enforcement remain outside motivational arbitration.

The motivational system cannot grant permission, weaken those boundaries, or reinterpret them as negotiable values.

## Outcome learning

After a successful turn, Ari stores only a compact strategy-level motivational reflection:

- which side won;
- the dominant drive;
- the competing value;
- the deterministic rationale code;
- whether the outcome was better or worse than expected;
- whether future balance should shift slightly toward drive permission, restraint, or remain unchanged.

No hidden chain-of-thought is stored.

Positive outcomes after allowing a drive can increase future drive permission slightly. Poor outcomes after excessive restraint can do the same. Conversely, poor drive outcomes and successful restraint can shift the balance modestly toward restraint.

Adaptive bias is capped so a short streak cannot permanently dominate Ari.

## Explainability

The latest compact motive/outcome reflection is carried into the next cognitive workspace.

If asked why she made a recent choice, Ari can explain the strategy-level reason and observed consequence without inventing feelings or exposing hidden reasoning.

## Relationship to the Discovery Lab

The Discovery Lab Motivational Conflict Core remains an isolated experimental mechanism.

Production Ari does **not** install that deterministic synthetic impulse generator. Instead, production arbitration derives competing drives from existing curiosity, reward learning, functional affect, self-adaptation, conscience signals, and outcome history.

The Lab remains the causal test bed; production uses the bounded, outcome-adaptive arbitration policy.
