# ARI Circle V6 — Launch Place Seed

The first Explore inventory is deliberately small and service-curated.

## V1 policy

- Seed only public destinations with a clear first-party municipal source.
- Keep Place coordinates about the destination itself; never copy or infer a user's live location into the Place catalog.
- Mark launch rows `curated`, `active`, and `safe_public_place = true` only when they are appropriate public discovery destinations.
- Store source provenance in `metadata.source_url` so future curation can be audited.
- Do not rank Places by engagement, popularity, payment, premium status, followers, likes, or views.
- Add/retire destinations through reviewed migrations or a future privileged curation surface, not authenticated client table writes.

## San Diego V1

The initial inventory uses four City of San Diego-managed destinations spanning central park, waterfront, and trail/open-space use cases. This is seed inventory, not a claim of exhaustive coverage.

Before expanding to another launch area, verify the public operator/source, coordinates, activity affordances, and current status of each destination.
